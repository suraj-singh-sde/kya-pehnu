# LinkedIn post draft

I got tired of standing in front of my almirah asking "kya pehnu?" every morning, so I built an AI stylist for it, and I'm open-sourcing it today.

Kya Pehnu (क्या पहनूँ):
• Photograph your clothes, a vision model tags every piece and builds your digital almirah
• Outfits planned around the weather, the occasion (hello, Diwali) and what you wore last week
• A chat stylist that knows exactly what you own, and never tells you to go shop

Bring your own key. No hosted service, no subscription. `docker compose up`, paste your Claude / OpenAI / Gemini / OpenRouter key (or run Ollama locally), done. Your photos stay on your machine and go only to the provider you pick.
